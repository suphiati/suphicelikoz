About Me

From engineering to logistics,
from logistics to software

I started my professional career as a Mining Engineer and permanent site supervisor. Between 2015 and 2019 I worked in field operations in the mining industry, where I learned technical processes, occupational safety and what regulation actually means on the ground.

In 2019 I moved into Dangerous Goods Safety Advisory. I worked on air and road transport of dangerous goods at several organisations, including FedEx Express, applying ADR and IATA DGR regulations inside daily operations rather than only reading them.

“Most of the problems I ran into in the field were, in fact, software problems. The day I realised that, I changed direction.”

Recurring operational mistakes, lost paperwork and manual checks could largely be solved with software. So I started building that capability: I studied Computer Programming and earned certifications in software development and testing to put a proper technical foundation under the practice.

In 2026 I founded my own software company. I now develop products that bring together dangerous goods safety, logistics, transport and software technology — using both my industry experience and my engineering skills to ship my own products.

SafeCargo

safecargo.io

A Turkish-language dangerous goods compliance platform bringing four transport modes under one roof: ADR (road), RID (rail), IMDG (sea) and IATA DGR (air).

ProblemFour separate regimes, four separate sources, nearly all of them in English. Checking a single UN number across all four modes takes hours.
Solution110+ interactive pages, unified search across all four regimes, 3500+ entry Table A and DG List lookup, segregation matrix, lithium battery decision tree, 1000-point calculator, tunnel categories and checklists.
QualityUnit and end-to-end test coverage, a 149-URL dynamic sitemap, CSP/HSTS security headers.
